About our PTA

Parents and teachers on the same team

Maple Grove Elementary PTA is 412 member families who give an hour where they can, so every child in Springfield's Maple Grove gets more from school.

What we do

Small hours that add up to a better school year

We raise money for what the school budget can't cover, run the family events kids remember, and give every parent an easy way in. Whether you have one hour a year or one a week, there is a place for you.

  • Open to every family and staff member
  • Budget voted on by members each September
  • Newsletter in English and Spanish

The board

Five volunteers who keep things running

Elected by members every May for one school year. Each one is a parent here, and each one answers email within two school days.

How decisions get made

Every dollar is voted on by parents

  1. 01

    Any parent can suggest

    Send an idea to the board or bring it to a meeting. Teachers send requests through the grant form each September.

  2. 02

    The board drafts a budget

    The treasurer and principal match ideas to what the school year's fundraising can cover.

  3. 03

    Members vote in September

    Every member family gets one vote, in the room or by video link.

  4. 04

    We report back monthly

    The treasurer shares every dollar spent at each monthly meeting.
