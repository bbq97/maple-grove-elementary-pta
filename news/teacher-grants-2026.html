Teacher grants: 14 classrooms get new supplies

Your dues and the spring auction paid for $5,600 in teacher grants this fall: wobble stools, science kits and a class set of graphic novels.

19 Oct 2026

Every September, Maple Grove teachers can ask the PTA for up to $400 for something their class needs. This year 14 teachers applied and every request was funded.

The list includes wobble stools for a 2nd-grade room, two science kits for 4th grade and a class set of graphic novels for the 5th-grade book club.

Thank you to every family who paid dues and bid at the spring auction. The grants are part of the $38,600 raised last school year (sample figures for this template).
